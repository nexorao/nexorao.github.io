FAQ
Good questions

  

    
Do you resell consumer subscriptions?
No. NEXORA uses only official provider APIs with server-held keys.

    
Does MCP unlock other providers’ apps?
No. MCP is an integration protocol; it does not unlock consumer subscriptions or proprietary internal tools.

    
When are credits granted after payment?
Only after server-side signature verification and verified webhooks, never on a browser-side success message.
